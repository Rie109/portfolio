Journal Article • In Review 2026

Dynamic RDS(on) Trapping Kinetics and Gate-Lag Degradation in High-Voltage AlGaN/GaN HEMTs Under Severe Electric Field Stress

Quan (Harry) Nguyen, Joint Semiconductor Research Group, Foxconn Semiconductor Research Center & NYCU

IEEE Transactions on Electron Devices (TED) — Manuscript in Peer Review

Abstract & Technical Summary ▾

High-voltage gallium nitride (GaN) high-electron-mobility transistors (HEMTs) suffer from dynamic on-resistance (RDS(on)) degradation induced by electron trapping in buffer layers and surface states during off-state bias. In this work, high-speed double-pulse switching diagnostics and pulsed I-V characterization reveal the activation energies and emission time constants of donor- and acceptor-like traps. A multi-field-plate architecture is proposed and validated through Sentaurus TCAD simulations, achieving a 34% reduction in peak electric field at the gate edge and limiting dynamic RDS(on) rise to under 1.18× across 600V switching cycles.

Conference Proceeding 2026

Switching Reliability and Body-Diode Degradation in 1.2 kV SiC Power MOSFETs Subjected to Repetitive Double-Pulse Stress

Quan (Harry) Nguyen, Advanced Power Device Group

IEEE Applied Power Electronics Conference and Exposition (APEC 2026)

Abstract & Technical Summary ▾

Silicon Carbide (SiC) power MOSFETs offer superior critical breakdown field and thermal conductivity for medium-voltage electric vehicle inverters. However, bipolar degradation in the intrinsic body diode caused by stacking faults can degrade forward voltage drop and switching loss over time. This paper presents an automated inductive double-pulse testbench operating at 800V / 40A with real-time junction temperature tracking, evaluating body-diode commutation ruggedness and safe operating area (SOA) boundaries.

Symposium Proceeding 2025

Numerical Field-Plate Optimization and Edge-Termination for Nanoscale High-Breakdown GaN Power Diodes

Quan (Harry) Nguyen, University of Cincinnati & NYCU Joint Semiconductor Lab

IEEE International Symposium on Power Semiconductor Devices and ICs (ISPSD)

Abstract & Technical Summary ▾

Edge termination efficiency directly dictates reverse blocking capability in vertical and lateral GaN power diodes. Utilizing Silvaco Atlas and Sentaurus TCAD 2D/3D numerical drift-diffusion simulations, junction termination extensions (JTE) combined with sub-micron dual field plates were simulated to smooth electric field crowding at anode perimeters. Experimental simulations demonstrate breakdown voltages exceeding 1400V with 94% junction efficiency.

Technical Whitepaper 2027

Hardware Security and Side-Channel Resilience for Microcontroller-Controlled Power Conversion Topologies

Quan (Harry) Nguyen, UC eCTF MITRE Engineering Team

MITRE Embedded System Challenge (eCTF) Technical Whitepaper Series

Abstract & Technical Summary ▾

As power electronics become tightly coupled with digital communication busses and IoT energy grids, cyber-physical security at the hardware layer is paramount. This whitepaper details physical side-channel power analysis (DPA) countermeasures, cryptographic key protection inside isolated flash sectors, and hardware-enforced memory protection units (MPU) for power converters under malicious firmware injection attacks.
